Construction Cost Calculator
Privacy Policy

Published by Decor Designs. Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Preliminary estimates

Calculations support preliminary quantity and budget planning. Default prices and material coefficients are examples, not live market prices. Verify quantities, rates, structural details and site conditions with qualified professionals. The app does not provide a structural design or a complete tender BOQ.

Information on your device

Calculator entries, saved rates, projects, purchase and usage logs, company details and your chosen logo are stored locally. There is no account, login or developer-operated cloud service. The app disables Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup. We do not receive your project or company data.

Photos and PDF sharing

You may select one logo through Android's document picker. The app does not request access to your photo library. PDF reports are created in local cache. When you choose Share, the selected receiving app gets the report, including any company details you added; that service's privacy policy applies.

Advertising

Google AdMob uses the internet to serve and measure ads. Its SDK may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, device or other identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as crash and performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not sell the project data you enter. Google processes advertising data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices and retention

Where required, a Google consent form appears before ads are requested. You can revisit available choices using the button below. You can manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Delete projects in the app, remove your logo, or clear app storage to erase local data. Uninstalling removes app-private data; PDFs already shared or saved elsewhere remain with their recipients. Google's retention and deletion choices are described in its privacy policy.

Audience and security

This app is intended for adults planning construction. It is not directed to children. Local data is protected by Android's app storage controls. Ad traffic uses encryption in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Decor Designs at info@interiordecordesigns.in. Policy updates will be published with a revised date.
